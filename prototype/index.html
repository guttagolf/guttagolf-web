    FAQ

    Good to know

    How many people can play in one bay?

    Up to 4 players per bay. The rate is for the bay, so it is the same price for 1 player or 4.

    How far ahead can I book?

    Up to 7 days ahead. Members can book up to 10 days ahead.

    How do I pay?

    You pay when you book, by Visa, Mastercard or Amex. You can also use coupon hours.

    Can I cancel or change my booking?

    Cancel at least 24 hours before your start time. A 4% processing fee applies to refunds.

    Do I need to bring clubs?

    Bring your own clubs. Please don't bring your own golf balls; the auto tee-up uses ours.

    Can I bring food or drinks?

    Light snacks and non-alcoholic drinks are fine. No alcohol, smoking or vaping.

    Is parking free?

    Yes, parking is free for all players.

    Do you host tournaments or company events?

    Yes. Email info@guttagolf.ca with your date and group size and we'll put a plan together.
