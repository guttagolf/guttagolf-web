Before you book

Good to know

  • Bring your own clubs
  • Please don't bring golf balls; the auto tee-up uses ours
  • Cancel at least 24 hours ahead for a refund (4% processing fee)
  • Paying at Gutta? Call or text us if you can't make it, so someone else can play

  • No alcohol, smoking or vaping
