Booking and paying

How far ahead can I book?

Up to 7 days ahead online. Members can text us to reserve further ahead.

How do I pay?

Pay online when you book (card, Apple Pay or Google Pay), or choose Pay at Gutta and pay by card or cash when you arrive. To use coupon hours, call or text us to book.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Call or text us with your booking code at least 24 hours before your start time. Online payments are refunded less a 4% processing fee. If you chose Pay at Gutta, please still let us know so someone else can play.

Can I play late at night?

Yes. Late night (12 AM – 6 AM) is by reservation only: book by 9 PM the same day. We are closed 6 AM – 9 AM.

At the bay

How many people can play in one bay?

Up to 4 players per bay. The rate is for the bay, so it's the same price for 1 player or 4.

Do I need to bring clubs?

Bring your own clubs. Please don't bring your own golf balls; the auto tee-up uses ours.

Are there bays for left-handed players?

Yes. Five of our eight bays work for left- and right-handed players. The booking page shows which bays are right-handed only (Bays 1, 7 and 8).

Can I bring food or drinks?

Light snacks and non-alcoholic drinks are fine. No alcohol, smoking or vaping.

Is parking free?

Yes, parking is free for all players.

Lessons and events

How much are lessons?

$100 for a 1-hour private lesson, or $800 for a 10-lesson package. See Lessons.

Do you host tournaments or company events?

Yes. Email info@guttagolf.ca with your date and group size and we'll put a plan together.
