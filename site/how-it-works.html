How it works

Book, check in, swing

Your first visit, step by step. No app and no membership needed.

  1. Book online

    • Open the booking page on any phone or computer
    • Pick a day, how long you want to play and a start time
    • Choose any open bay

  2. Pay and get your confirmation

    • Pay by Visa, Mastercard or Amex
    • Or use hours from a coupon pack
    • A confirmation email arrives right away

  3. Check in and play

    • Say hi at the front desk; we'll take you to your bay
    • Choose driving range or a course
    • The ball tees up by itself after every shot
