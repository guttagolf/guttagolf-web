How lessons work

Your first lesson

  1. Tell us your goal

    Send the form above or call us. A score target, a slice to fix, or just getting started are all good goals.

  2. Swing on the simulator

    Your coach watches your swing and the shot data together, so every tip comes with a number.

  3. Practice between lessons

    Book a bay on your own and work on the drill. Coupon hours make it cheaper.
