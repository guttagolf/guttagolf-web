Privacy & booking terms

Plain-language rules for your details and your booking. Questions? Contact us.

Privacy

What we collect

  • Your name and phone number, and your email if you give it or pay online
  • Your booking: day, time, bay and price
  • Payment results from Stripe (paid or not, and the amount). We never see or store your card number
  • If you choose to tell us: your postal code, age range and gender
  • Whether you agreed to get deals and news by text, and when
  • Which promo code or link brought you to book
  • Anonymous website statistics: pages viewed, the site you came from and your device type, under a random ID. These are not linked to your name or phone number
  • Technical records such as your IP address, kept to stop misuse of the booking system

Why we use it

  • To hold your bay, recognise you when you book again, and contact you about your booking
  • To take payment and give refunds
  • To keep our tax and business records
  • To understand which neighbourhoods and groups we serve and which offers work, using totals rather than individual profiles

We don't sell your details. We only text you deals and news if you tick the box to agree when you book. Every marketing text names Gutta Golf, and you can reply STOP at any time to stop them. Otherwise we only email or text you about your bookings.

Who handles it

Card payments are processed by Stripe. Bookings are stored with Supabase, texts are sent through Twilio, and the website runs on Vercel. Our team uses an AI assistant (run through ElevenLabs) to read booking and website totals for planning; it is not given your phone number. These providers may store data outside Canada and must keep it secure.

How long we keep it

Booking and payment records are kept as long as Canadian tax rules require (usually six years). Technical records are deleted sooner.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details by emailing info@guttagolf.ca. We may need to keep records the law requires.

Booking terms

  • One booking is one private bay for up to 4 players. The price is per bay, before 13% HST
  • Bookings open up to 7 days ahead. Late night (12 AM – 6 AM) must be booked by 9 PM the same day
  • Pay now: you pay online when you book
  • Pay at Gutta: your bay is held and you pay by card or cash when you arrive. One phone number can hold up to 2 unpaid bookings. If you often don't show up, we may ask you to pay online in future
  • To cancel, call or text us with your booking code at least 24 hours before your start time. Online payments are refunded less a 4% processing fee. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice are not refunded
  • Your time ends at the booked finish time, even if you arrive late
  • Bring your own clubs. Golf balls are provided. No alcohol, smoking or vaping
  • You are responsible for damage you or your group cause to the bay or equipment beyond normal use
